# music

Press 1 or 2 to play one of two music tracks; M turns the music on and off.

It shows the `music` engine library: `playMusicOnKey` loads the track of each `PlayOnKey`
(`ctx.music.load`, with the URL `ctx.assets.getAsset` gives for the asset) and plays it when its
key is pressed (`ctx.music.play`). Unlike sound effects, only one track plays at a time: starting
a track pauses the one playing, and pressing its key again resumes it where it stopped.

The music library **starts muted**, and `ctx.music.mute()` toggles it: press M once to hear
anything. `MuteOnKey` keeps track of the state, which the library does not expose.

`MusicLibrary` 3.0.0 has no pause, stop or loop: a track plays once to its end, and muting it
does not pause it.

```sh
pnpm install
pnpm dev      # play it
pnpm editor   # open it in the editor
```

`assets/track-a.wav` and `assets/track-b.wav` are sine-wave melodies generated by a script for this
example: no third-party material. `image-fill.ts`, the `Position2D`, `DrawableRect2D`,
`DrawableCircle2D` and `DrawableText2D` components and the `draw2D` system are copies of
`shared/2d`, and the `PlayOnKey` and `MuteOnKey` components copies of `shared/basics` (see the
repository's README).
